# Changelog

The Grounnel and Biassemble website. One release per merged pull request: a feature bumps the middle
number, a fix-only release bumps the last. Pull requests that only changed docs are not releases.
Versions before 0.15.0 were assigned on 2026-11-13 from the merge history. The engine behind the site
has its own changelog in biassemble-core.

## 0.15.0 — 2026-11-13

- A Contacts page with our email and X account, on both sites.
- Grounnel links to Biassemble again, from the menu, the footer and the About page.
- Links to a section of the About page open at that section; the assistant setup is at /about#mcp.
- The footer shows the site and engine versions.

## 0.14.0 — 2026-11-13

- An About section on using Grounnel from an AI assistant, over MCP.
- The own-key wording says the key is held encrypted for at most 45 minutes while a check runs.

## 0.13.0 — 2026-11-01

- The Stats page shows test-set consistency and a three-model comparison.

## 0.12.1 — 2026-10-27

- Fixes to how source text and a finished check are shown.

## 0.12.0 — 2026-10-26

- The pasted key can be shown or hidden.

## 0.11.0 — 2026-10-25
